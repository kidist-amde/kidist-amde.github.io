I am a PhD researcher in the [Information Retrieval Lab](https://irlab.science.uva.nl/) at the University of Amsterdam. I am supervised by Prof. dr. [Maarten de Rijke](https://staff.fnwi.uva.nl/m.derijke/) and Dr. [Andrew Yates](https://andrewyates.net/).

My research focuses on generative methods for information retrieval. I study how retrieval systems can generate, rank, recommend, and adapt to information needs across languages and domains, with an emphasis on generative retrieval, neural information retrieval, recommender systems, robust decoding, evaluation, and continual learning for retrieval systems. [[Google Scholar]](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=_c20PQwAAAAJ&hl=en) [[GitHub]](https://github.com/kidist-amde) [[LinkedIn]](https://www.linkedin.com/in/kidistamde/) [[ResearchGate]](https://www.researchgate.net/profile/Kidist-Amde-Mekonnen?ev=hdr_xprf) [[CV]](/files/CV_Kidist_Amde_Mekonnen_2026_meta.pdf)

### Research Interests
* Generative information retrieval and generative modeling for retrieval
* Neural information retrieval and recommender systems
* Multilingual and low-resource retrieval
* Continual learning for retrieval systems
* Robust decoding and evaluation for generative retrieval
* Retrieval-augmented generation and large-scale machine learning
* Multimodal learning and reinforcement learning for retrieval and recommendation

### Research Highlights
* **Continual generative retrieval:** developing parametric-memory approaches that let generative retrieval systems update and retain document knowledge over time.
* **Decoding and reproducibility:** stress-testing look-ahead decoding priors to clarify when generative retrieval gains are robust.
* **Direct relevance optimization:** designing lightweight objectives for document relevance in generative information retrieval.
* **Amharic passage retrieval:** building text embeddings and benchmarks for multilingual and low-resource retrieval evaluation.
