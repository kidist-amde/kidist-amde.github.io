I am a PhD Researcher at the University of Amsterdam's IRLab. My research focuses on generative retrieval, recommendation, neural information retrieval, multilingual retrieval, retrieval-augmented generation, and large-scale machine learning.

I study how retrieval systems can represent, generate, rank, and adapt to information needs across languages and domains. My work connects generative information retrieval with efficient learning objectives, continual memory, and evaluation for real-world retrieval settings. [[Google Scholar]](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=_c20PQwAAAAJ&hl=en) [[GitHub]](https://github.com/kidist-amde) [[LinkedIn]](https://www.linkedin.com/in/kidistamde/) [[CV]](/CV_Kidist_Amde_Mekonnen_2026_meta.pdf)

### Research Highlights
* **Continual generative retrieval:** developing parametric-memory approaches that let generative retrieval systems update and retain document knowledge over time.
* **Decoding and reproducibility:** stress-testing look-ahead decoding priors to clarify when generative retrieval gains are robust.
* **Direct relevance optimization:** designing lightweight objectives for document relevance in generative information retrieval.
* **Amharic passage retrieval:** building text embeddings and benchmarks for multilingual and low-resource retrieval evaluation.
