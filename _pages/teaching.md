### Thesis Supervision

[**BSc Thesis Supervision, University of Amsterdam, 2026**](/teaching/2026-bsc-thesis-supervision)

Supervising a BSc thesis on multilingual search for low-resource languages, including Telugu, Amharic, Swahili, and Bengali. The project investigates how lexical, dense, and hybrid retrieval methods perform across languages with different scripts, linguistic characteristics, and resource levels. It examines query translation, multilingual representation learning, tokenization, and retrieval effectiveness, supported by detailed error analysis to identify where multilingual retrieval systems succeed or fail.
